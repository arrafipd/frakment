Add holo-card module: a card with a procedural foil that reacts to pointer tilt and device motion. Two variants: `card` (default) with a chip and an optional `logo`, and `image` with artwork under the foil. Two presets: `bursts` and `cosmos`. `logo` accepts a URL, plain text, or any ReactNode. `chip` overrides the built-in EMV chip.
