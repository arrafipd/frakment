Chain of Thought is a summary line that expands into the steps an agent took. The summary says what happened, like "Researched in 3 steps". The steps sit inside it, each one a line of its own.

### Full demo

The preview on this page shows the chain-of-thought primitive composing with `useChat`, `createChat`, and `transport` from `@frakment/ai`. It streams three tool calls with a line-by-line build log and uses nested `TraceCollapsible` for auto-expand and manual toggle.

The composition pulls `@ai-sdk/react`, `ai`, `badge`, `bubble`, `button`, `code-block`, `message`, and `trace`. Follow the [Chat tutorial](/docs/ai-elements/chat) for the wiring pattern, or replace `createChat` with your own backend that streams `data-buildLog` and tool parts.

## Accessibility

- **TraceIcon** is `aria-hidden`. **TraceTitle** is the accessible name. Write it as a sentence: "Searched for minimumReleaseAge", not just "minimumReleaseAge".
- Status is conveyed by icon shape and color, not color alone.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: shimmer stops automatically.
- Use `open={open ?? running}` so a reader's manual toggle wins over the auto state.

## Anatomy

```tsx title="chain-of-thought.tsx"
<ChainOfThought>
  <ChainOfThoughtHeader />
  <ChainOfThoughtContent>
    <ChainOfThoughtStep />
  </ChainOfThoughtContent>
</ChainOfThought>
```

- **ChainOfThought** — collapsible root
- **ChainOfThoughtHeader** — summary line ("Researched in 3 steps")
- **ChainOfThoughtContent** — step list
- **ChainOfThoughtStep** — one step with a Trace line

## Best Practices

- **One summary per turn.** The header is a single line, not a tree.
- **Keep steps atomic.** One action per step.
- **Collapse by default.** Steps expand on demand.
- **Keep nesting shallow.** Two levels is enough.
